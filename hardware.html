How to check your laptop

  1. Windows: press the Windows key, type System information, and open it. Look at Processor and Installed RAM.
  2. Mac: click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. Look at Chip or Processor and Memory.
  3. Storage: open Settings, then Storage (Windows) or General, then Storage (Mac).
  4. Not sure about a result? Send us a screenshot and we will tell you if it works.
